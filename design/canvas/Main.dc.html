Fortnite Creative · Training Ground · Master plan
One hub, six sealed drill levels
80 × 45 tiles (about 410 × 230 m). Each level is a closed box sized to its drill and nothing larger, so there is no wasted space and no ambiguity about where to stand or what to do.

Hub · Spawn
Training Hub
20 × 12 tiles
Drill selector · Loadout locker · Stats wall · Open warm-up pad
Progression. Play A → F in order the first time. Later, the hub selector jumps straight to any level.
Circulation. One door per level, linked to the hub by a single corridor. No level opens onto another.
Scale. 6 levels, 28 stations. 1 grid square = 1 build tile. White square = player spawn.
