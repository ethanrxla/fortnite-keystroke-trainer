Props and devices
Each bay is sealed and resets on its own. Bay 2 uses a closing barrier to simulate the storm. Bots spawn from one of three points at random so players cannot memorise the setup.
Bots
Bays 1 and 4 use tier 2 bots (build, no edit). Bays 2 and 3 use tier 3 (build and edit). Bots reuse the tiers defined in Level E, so one bot setup serves both levels.
