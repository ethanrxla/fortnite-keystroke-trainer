Props and devices
Kill ceiling at 8 tiles, flat floor, solid outer walls. Round ends at 5 eliminations or 3 minutes. Both players respawn 3 seconds after an elimination and all pieces clear every round.
Bots
One spawner on the far side, set per tier from the ready room. The bot never leaves the arena. The ready room locks the tier once the round starts.

Run flow
