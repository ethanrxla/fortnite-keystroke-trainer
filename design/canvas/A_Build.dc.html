Door · to hub
Dashed box = 3×3 envelope. White dot = start position.
Props and devices
Each stall: floor decal for the envelope, no-build barrier on the stall edge, kill ceiling 5 tiles up, start pad, sign with task and pass mark. Stall 6 and 7 add a button that rebuilds the starting pad after each run.
Bots
None in stalls 1 to 5, so the player focuses on muscle memory. Optional tier 1 dummy at the back of stalls 6 and 7 that pings the box, to practise boxing up under light fire.

Run flow
1 Step on the start pad. 2 Timer starts on your first piece. 3 Finish when the sign's piece count is met. 4 Result shows on the sign, then everything resets. Targets are starting values: tune them with real players.
