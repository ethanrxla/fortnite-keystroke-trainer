Teach the why, then check the result
There is usually a better way, and players can be creative inside it. Every drill states one principle, the reason for it, and where the player is free to choose.
1 · BRIEF
Rule and why
The cue shows the principle and its reason for 7 seconds before rep one.
2 · PREDICT
Call your score
Three buttons: low, medium, high. The card compares it to the real result.
3 · DO
Drill under the beat
Pressure and exposure do the grading. No nagging mid-set.
4 · DIAGNOSE
Band and tip
Learning, cleaning, solid or sharp, plus one coaching line and the hits taken.
5 · REFLECT
One question
For example: were both side walls up before you opened the window?
6 · PLATEAU
Stuck is normal
Four flat sets: drop one tempo and rebuild the habit slowly.

Stand on the pad · 10 reps
Gun, pickaxe and build switching
W1 gun swap, W2 pickaxe and gun, W3 build then gun. A swap counts only if the right tool's target registers a hit in the beat. A hit on the other tool's target means the wrong item was out. Latency is swap-to-hit time. Principle: right tool, ready early.
Where AI fits (a published island cannot call the internet)
Claude as author. Writes and refines the principle, why, tip and reflection text in one JSON file. Baked into the map, so it works for everyone.
ElevenLabs voice. Every brief and reflection line is rendered once offline, imported as audio, and played by an audio device. Same words as the signs.
Claude live coach. Optional, in the companion app on the player's PC. Reads the last set from the log and explains the errors in terms of the principle. Needs the player's own key.
Not in the map. No AI runs inside the island; fixed text teaches more consistently. NVIDIA is a drop-in alternative for either job if cost or latency requires it.
