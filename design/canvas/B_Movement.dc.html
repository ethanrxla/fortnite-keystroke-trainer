Props and devices
Floor decal every tile in lanes 1 and 2 so distance reads at a glance. Height-cap marker on lane 1 at 10 tiles, kill ceiling at 11. Tunnel lane ceiling at 4 tiles. Walls between lanes so shots and sound never leak.
Bots
A guard or sentry is wired to each lane's pressure trigger and fires at the start of every rep. Damage taken during a sealing step fails that step as exposed. Lane 4's bot also contests the wall for the claim-first drill (E6).
